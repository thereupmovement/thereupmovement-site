Who we are

The ReUp Movement is a Houston-based nonprofit built on a simple idea: everybody deserves a new beginning. Founded by Walter Barreda in 2017 and incorporated in 2018, ReUp gets furniture and household goods to families starting over, serves meals and essentials to people experiencing homelessness, cleans up neighborhoods carrying illegal dumping, and equips partner organizations abroad, starting in Belize City.

Our vision

To create families who are empowered to love unconditionally, communities who are united to achieve a common purpose, and a world whose people truly enjoy life.

What we do

Four ways we serve

ReUp SuppliesFurniture, clothing and household goods for families starting over, here at home.
ReUp ServesHot meals, hygiene kits and direct outreach for community guests.
ReUp CleanNeighborhood cleanups that take on illegal dumping. Same block, cleaner streets.
ReUp InternationalDonated goods for partner organizations abroad, starting in Belize City.
ReUp International

What we gather in Houston, our partners put to work in Belize City.

Our partner in Belize City, God Ordained & Established, uses the goods we send to serve and train its own community. Container 2 is being packed now.
