When you get there

1
Find the event lead.Get your spot. Stay for the five-minute huddle before the line opens.
2
Tables go with our backs to a wall or fence.Goods sit behind us. Guests only come from the front of the table.
3
Set it up like a supermarket.Same items together, in the same order, easy to hand across.
4
Know the bundle.The event lead sets what each household gets, so the last family in line gets the same as the first.

At the check-in table

Check in every guest and volunteer. Then hand guests the needs form. Nobody needs ID, and nobody is turned away for skipping a question.

Spots on the line

Check-in tableEvery guest checks in. Hand out the needs form here too.
Head of the lineKeeps the line calm and moving.
Behind the lineHands goods to each guest.
End of the lineRestocks the table. Ask for help before it runs out.

Rules on the line

1
Know your spot.Ask the event lead where you stand before it starts.
2
Leaving the line? Say so.Tell the person next to you where you're going.
3
Hand goods to each guest.Don't leave piles out for people to grab.
4
Trouble with a guest? Get Walter or Charles.Don't step in yourself.
